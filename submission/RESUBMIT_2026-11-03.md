## 2. LinkedIn

```
I've resubmitted Soundstage to Comfy's Open Source Higgsfield challenge.

Soundstage is a film set inside ComfyUI. Most AI video tools give you a prompt box and a set of one-click effects. Soundstage gives you a 3D stage first: you block the scene, and the video models follow that blocking shot for shot.

Since my first submission on 30 October I've shipped 29 milestones. The ones I'm proudest of:

- People and horses that move properly. Keyed people walk, jog and run with feet that stay planted. Horses walk, trot, canter and gallop, and riders post the trot and ride two-point at the gallop.
- Everything on the set collides. Cars crash into each other instead of passing through, flip at speed, knock down street lamps and stop at walls (or break through a weak one). The bake is deterministic: scrub back and the same frames play.
- Physics and destruction. Crates fracture into splintered boards where they're hit, cardboard crushes and stays crushed, and there is cloth, rope and sand that slumps when a car drives through.
- Movie stunts on real vehicle dynamics. The reverse J-turn and the handbrake 180 come from a four-wheel car simulation with Pacejka tyres and weight transfer, not hand-placed keys. A new Racing Cinema look and racing camera presets (bumper, over the tyre, arm car, long-lens pan) are made for them.
- Drive mode. Select a person, a car, a horse or a boat, press D and control it with the keyboard or a game controller. Walk to a car, get in and drive. Record the take and it becomes animation on the shot. You can even take over a car in the middle of a keyed stunt, and every take you keep is cleaned up automatically (spikes, teleports, impossible speeds) and fitted with far fewer keys.
- Boats and swimming. Nine boats on a simulated wave field, divers who kick at a realistic rate, and swimmers doing freestyle and breaststroke.
- Real performances. Film yourself on a webcam or phone; your body, face and voice drive the character, with lip sync measured at 0.92 mouth correlation. Voice conversion turns your voice into the character's, so one person can perform every role.
- Video to 3D set. A clip becomes an editable stage: the location, the tracked camera and the performer.
- A one-click layer. A ✦ One-click button on every take: effects (weather, time of day, cutouts), relighting, a new camera angle, expanding to new aspect ratios, a seamless loop with a seam meter that shows whether the join is visible, a reshoot from a new angle, Open in ComfyUI to see the exact graph behind the take, and filming a move on your phone. Each render is saved as a new take.
- Tools for the hours between renders. ✦ Ask, an assistant on every screen, answers "how do I…?" with steps it can point to (Show me) or do for you, with Undo. The Activity bar names the node your render is on (sampling step 8 of 12, the depth map, the VAE decode) with time left, and the node that failed if one does. Passes opens a take to judge it by eye: the alpha matte's soft edge measured in pixels, the pose read with per-point confidence, the mouth against the sound, and the depth. And the editor now plays your takes in real time.

The pipeline runs in production order: script, cast, set, frame, motion, review, deliver. It ends on a real timeline with transitions and generated sound, and exports to Avid, Premiere and Resolve.

It runs locally on open weights by default and is MIT licensed.

If you're judging, the README now opens with "Judging it? Start here": a short path through the browser demo, which has a new tour of what's new, and a table of what runs live and what is replayed from a real run.
```

**What's new since the first submission**

```
I first submitted on 30 October (08:29 PDT). Since then: 29 milestones, every one logged in CHANGELOG.md.

A real 3D film set
- People walk, jog and run with foot-locked gaits (planted feet, two-bone IK). Horses walk, trot, canter and gallop with true footfalls; riders sit the walk, post the trot and ride two-point at the gallop, and a mount is a real one-second climb into the saddle.
- Everything collides: cars, people, street lamps, benches, hydrants, trees and walls are bodies with real masses. Keyed cars crash instead of passing through, cars flip above a speed threshold, poles get knocked down, a solid wall stops a car and a weak one breaks. The bake is deterministic: scrubbing back plays the same frames, and editing a key re-bakes. Before the Director renders a shot, it checks for anything passing through something else and offers Crash them / Reroute.
- Physics and destruction: real convex collision shapes, Voronoi fracture with a constraint network that bends and cracks before it breaks, splintered render meshes, one particle system for dust, debris and tyre smoke, and soft bodies (cloth, ropes, a rope bridge, cardboard, sand piles). CPU XPBD by default, GPU (AVBD) in beta.
- Movie stunts on vehicle dynamics: reverse J-turn, handbrake 180 and donut are driver scripts on a four-wheel car sim with Pacejka tyres, wheel spin and weight transfer; the body rolls and pitches on its springs. A Racing Cinema look and racing camera presets (Bumper cam, Over the tyre, Arm car tracking, Long-lens pan) are built for them.
- Drive mode: press D to control a person, car, horse, bike or boat with keyboard, mouse or a game controller. Get into a car or onto a horse, pick up and throw a crate. ● REC records the take as keys on the shot, layered like a take recorder. Driving a keyed object takes over from its keys (a car mid J-turn drives instead of sticking), and each kept take is auto-corrected (spikes, teleports, top speed, roll, ground contact) and fitted with far fewer keys, with Undo to the raw take. ⌂ Back to the set (.) and ↶ Last view (,) bring a lost camera home.
- Boats: nine watercraft (speedboats, fishing boat, sailing dinghy and yacht, canoe, kayak, rowboat, whitewater and log rafts) on a JONSWAP wave field, with planing, sailing heel and paddle strokes; Lake and River rapids sets.
- Swimming: divers flutter-kick, frog-kick and hover at a cadence set from the Strouhal number; swimmers do freestyle, breaststroke and tread water; the reef gets caustics and light shafts.
- Full control over every object (Parts): doors, lamps, light bars, steering, suspension, a horse's head and gait, lens focus and T-stop, all keyable.

Script to screen
- Script → cast → shots → timeline: the Cut is now a real editing timeline with dissolves, dips and wipes, sound generated in place on A1–A4, auto-duck, loudness targets, subtitles, titles and motion graphics, rendering MP4, ProRes 422 HQ or 9:16 and exporting to Avid, Premiere, Resolve and FCP.
- The Director's Book (a binder page per scene) and Pitch Mode (talk the scene through and the set builds itself, a sketch per beat).
- Quality presets Draft · Final · Hollywood (2624×1408), and Adherence Loose · Balanced · Exact so renders stay on the blocking.

Real performances
- Record or import an actor: DWPose reads body, hands and 68 face landmarks; the voice drives lip sync through LTX-2.5's audio mask (mouth correlation 0.92, 0-frame lag).
- Voice conversion with Chatterbox VC: keep your voice or convert it to the character's, so one performer can act every part.

Video → 3D
- Drop a clip and it is rebuilt as an editable 3D set: location, tracked camera, set pieces and the motion-captured performer.

Checks and the one-click layer
- ✦ One-click: 52 cards on every take and Frame candidate (Nightfall, Rain, Snow, Fog, Golden hour, the 18 lighting setups, Cutout with ProRes 4444 alpha, new angles, Expand to six aspects), plus Seamless loop with a seam meter that reports whether the join shows, Reshoot from a new angle, Open in ComfyUI (the exact graph inside any take, in a new tab) and Send a move from your phone. Experimental cards are labelled as such. Every render lands as a new take.
- Every take checks itself (freezes, jumps, face drift, cars facing the wrong way) and gets a "Follows your blocking" score; Hollywood takes also get a local realism judge.
- The Director: talk or type to the set (local Qwen3.5 4B, offline Whisper), and an MCP server so Claude Desktop or Claude Code can drive the open App. Every change is proposed first and undoable.
- For judges: the README opens with "Judging it? Start here", and the browser demo opens on clay becoming the shot, with a 10-stop tour of what's new.
- ✦ Ask: an assistant button on every step (Cmd/Ctrl+K). It knows where you are, answers how-to questions with steps and Show me (it lights up the real button), or does it for you through the Director, with Undo.
- Activity: a top-row pill and panel for every job on the GPU queue. It names the node a render is on in plain words (Sampling: stage 1 · 8/12, Depth map, Decoding video (VAE)), with sampler steps, cached nodes, time left and Cancel; a failure names its node.
- Passes: one viewer to judge a take by eye. Alpha matte (matte, over checker or a busy background, rubylith, and the soft-edge band measured in pixels), pose with per-point confidence, talking (waveform against the mouth-open curve) and depth, with wipe, flicker and zoom to 800 % on square pixels.
- Real-time playback in the Cut: takes play at true speed from short-GOP proxies the server makes, with Final / Draft / Clay badges. On a simulated laptop, dropped frames fell from 84-90 a minute to 6.
- Your phone as the viewfinder; round trip with Blender (measured IoU 0.99+), plus export scripts for Unreal, Maya and Nuke.
```

**How it uses ComfyUI**

```
Soundstage is a ComfyUI custom node pack plus an App-mode workflow. The whole pipeline runs as ComfyUI nodes and the UI is a ComfyUI frontend extension, so every step is a graph you can open and edit.

- Frame: FLUX 3 Klein paints the first frame from the 3D stage's layout (Krea 2 locked to stage depth is optional).
- Motion: LTX-2.5 with the Layout-to-Render IC-LoRA (via ComfyUI-LTXVideo) animates each shot with sound, following the clay blocking and camera.
- Performances: LTX-2.5 with the union-control IC-LoRA and the audio noise mask for lip sync; Chatterbox for TTS and voice conversion.
- Motion capture: SAM 3D Body (ComfyUI core nodes). Mattes: SAM 3.1 and BiRefNet. Realism judge: Qwen3-VL through ComfyUI's TextGenerate.
- Renders go through ComfyUI's own queue, and its cache means each Run only does the new work. The Activity bar follows ComfyUI's own execution events, so it names the node running (a KSampler's steps, a VAE decode) and the node that failed.
- A Soundstage server route on ComfyUI makes playback proxies of your takes (short GOP, sized to the viewer), so the editor plays them in real time.
- Headless: tools/soundstage_sdk.py renders a saved shot through the Comfy SDK.
- Open weights by default; partner API nodes are hidden unless you start ComfyUI with SOUNDSTAGE_PARTNER_MODELS=1.
```
