**What's new since the first submission**

```
I first submitted on 30 October (08:29 PDT). Since then: 29 milestones, every one logged in CHANGELOG.md.

A real 3D film set
- People walk, jog and run with foot-locked gaits (planted feet, two-bone IK). Horses walk, trot, canter and gallop with true footfalls; riders sit the walk, post the trot and ride two-point at the gallop, and a mount is a real one-second climb into the saddle.
- Physics and destruction: real convex collision shapes, Voronoi fracture with a constraint network that bends and cracks before it breaks, splintered render meshes, one particle system for dust, debris and tyre smoke, and soft bodies (cloth, ropes, a rope bridge, cardboard, sand piles). CPU XPBD by default, GPU (AVBD) in beta.
- Movie stunts on vehicle dynamics: reverse J-turn, handbrake 180 and donut are driver scripts on a four-wheel car sim with Pacejka tyres, wheel spin and weight transfer; the body rolls and pitches on its springs.
- Drive mode: press D to control a person, car, horse, bike or boat with keyboard, mouse or a game controller. Get into a car or onto a horse, pick up and throw a crate. ● REC records the take as keys on the shot, layered like a take recorder.
- Boats: nine watercraft (speedboats, fishing boat, sailing dinghy and yacht, canoe, kayak, rowboat, whitewater and log rafts) on a JONSWAP wave field, with planing, sailing heel and paddle strokes; Lake and River rapids sets.
- Swimming: divers flutter-kick, frog-kick and hover at a cadence set from the Strouhal number; swimmers do freestyle, breaststroke and tread water; the reef gets caustics and light shafts.
- Full control over every object (Parts): doors, lamps, light bars, steering, suspension, a horse's head and gait, lens focus and T-stop, all keyable.

Script to screen
- Script → cast → shots → timeline: the Cut is now a real editing timeline with dissolves, dips and wipes, sound generated in place on A1–A4, auto-duck, loudness targets, subtitles, titles and motion graphics, rendering MP4, ProRes 422 HQ or 9:16 and exporting to Avid, Premiere, Resolve and FCP.
- The Director's Book (a binder page per scene) and Pitch Mode (talk the scene through and the set builds itself, a sketch per beat).
- Quality presets Draft · Final · Hollywood (2624×1408), and Adherence Loose · Balanced · Exact so renders stay on the blocking.

Real performances
- Record or import an actor: DWPose reads body, hands and 68 face landmarks; the voice drives lip sync through LTX-2.5's audio mask (mouth correlation 0.92, 0-frame lag).
- Voice conversion with Chatterbox VC: keep your voice or convert it to the character's, so one performer can act every part.

Video → 3D
- Drop a clip and it is rebuilt as an editable 3D set: location, tracked camera, set pieces and the motion-captured performer.

Checks and the one-click layer
- ✦ One-click: 52 cards on every take and Frame candidate (Nightfall, Rain, Snow, Fog, Golden hour, the 18 lighting setups, Cutout with ProRes 4444 alpha, new angles, Expand to six aspects). Experimental cards are labelled as such. Every result lands as a new take.
- Every take checks itself (freezes, jumps, face drift, cars facing the wrong way) and gets a "Follows your blocking" score; Hollywood takes also get a local realism judge.
- The Director: talk or type to the set (local Qwen3.5 4B, offline Whisper), and an MCP server so Claude Desktop or Claude Code can drive the open App. Every change is proposed first and undoable.
- Your phone as the viewfinder; round trip with Blender (measured IoU 0.99+), plus export scripts for Unreal, Maya and Nuke.
```

**How it uses ComfyUI**

```
Soundstage is a ComfyUI custom node pack plus an App-mode workflow. The whole pipeline runs as ComfyUI nodes and the UI is a ComfyUI frontend extension, so every step is a graph you can open and edit.

- Frame: FLUX 3 Klein paints the first frame from the 3D stage's layout (Krea 2 locked to stage depth is optional).
- Motion: LTX-2.5 with the Layout-to-Render IC-LoRA (via ComfyUI-LTXVideo) animates each shot with sound, following the clay blocking and camera.
- Performances: LTX-2.5 with the union-control IC-LoRA and the audio noise mask for lip sync; Chatterbox for TTS and voice conversion.
- Motion capture: SAM 3D Body (ComfyUI core nodes). Mattes: SAM 3.1 and BiRefNet. Realism judge: Qwen3-VL through ComfyUI's TextGenerate.
- Renders go through ComfyUI's own queue, and its cache means each Run only does the new work.
- Headless: tools/soundstage_sdk.py renders a saved shot through the Comfy SDK.
- Open weights by default; partner API nodes are hidden unless you start ComfyUI with SOUNDSTAGE_PARTNER_MODELS=1.
```

**Open-source licence**
