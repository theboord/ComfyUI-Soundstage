## LinkedIn

```
I built Soundstage for Comfy Org's Open Source Higgsfield challenge.

It is a ComfyUI node pack and App that works like a small film set:
1. Set: block the scene in 3D with actors, horses, cars, lights and several camera setups.
2. Frame: Krea 2 paints the first frame of every shot, steered by the stage's depth.
3. Motion: LTX-2.5 Layout-to-Render animates every shot with sound, following the blocking.
4. Review: compare clay, frame, draft and final on a wipe.
5. Deliver: one cut video, per-object layers for VFX, or an EDL/XML for your editor.

Everything is open source (MIT) and runs locally on open models.

Demo video: [VIDEO_LINK]
GitHub: [REPO_LINK]
```
