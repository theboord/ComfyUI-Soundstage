# Stage API for tools

Methods on the 3D stage (`web/stage/stage.mjs`, the `Stage` instance the App keeps as `node.ssStage`) for tools that edit a scene without a mouse: ✦ Ask's actions, scripts, tests. `duplicate`, `placeInFrame` and `setRig` are ordinary edits: on success each re-poses the stage and records one undo step (through `onChange`), like doing it by hand; a refused call changes nothing and records none. `openPhysicsPanel` only selects and opens a panel (no scene data changes), so it records no undo step, as selecting by hand records none. None of them throws for a bad argument (including `null` or a missing `where` for `placeInFrame`): they say what was wrong.

| Call | Does | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `stage.duplicate(id)` | A copy of object `id` (person, prop, vehicle) beside it: offset along +X by its height x 0.8 (at least 1 m), with its **base** keys (`keys`, deep-copied, offset the same), so in every shot without blocking of its own it moves like the original. Its per-shot blocking (`by_shot`) is not copied, even when the active shot has some. Does not need or change the selection. | the new id, or `null` when there is no object `id` |
| `stage.placeInFrame(id, {x, y?, distance_m?})` | Puts the object's ground point at a place in the **active shot camera's** frame (the shot's keys, rig and lens at the playhead, never a live virtual-camera preview on screen), on the walkable ground: the stage floor or the set's own raised floor (a station platform), so the point lands on the third at that floor's height. A person's feet follow the floor by themselves (key height 0), on a parked car, table or crate too (keyed at 0, they stand on it and follow it when it moves); only on a dock or a boat's deck over the water is a person keyed at its top (there 0 means in the water), and a prop floating in the water is never a place to stand; a prop on a raised floor is keyed at that floor's height (`key_y` set), and `key_y` is dropped only when none of its keys in any shot is raised any more. `x`: `"left"` or `"right"` (the thirds, 1/3 and 2/3 of the width), `"center"`, or a number 0..1 (0 = left edge), also as a numeric string (`"0.3"`); a boolean, an array or an empty string is refused. Riding (`mount_id`) or carried (`carry_id`) objects are refused (place what holds them). Under a steep or top-down camera the ground distance first falls then grows up the frame (the column passes under the camera): every crossing is found and the one nearest the frame's centre line is used, so near distances work there too. `distance_m`: how far from the camera, measured along the ground. `y` (0..1, 0 = top), used when there is no `distance_m`: where that screen point meets the ground. With neither, it keeps its present distance. A one-key object moves as a whole; a keyed (moving) one gets a key at the playhead. With per-shot blocking (`by_shot`) in the active shot, that shot's keys are edited and the base `keys` / `position` stay. | `{ok: true, position: [x, y, z]}` (y: 0, or the raised floor's height for a prop) or `{ok: false, error}` (unknown id, `where` not an object, bad `x` / `y` / `distance_m`, a point above the horizon, a distance nearer than the ground the camera sees, or farther than the camera can put it: a steep or top-down camera sees ground only so far up the frame, and the error says how far). `where` omitted or `null` = `{x: "center"}` at the present distance. |
| `stage.setRig(type, amount, params)` | Puts the active shot's camera on a rig (`free`, `tripod`, `dolly`, `chapman`, `slider`, `jib`, `steadicam`, `handheld`, `drone`, `drone_fpv`); `amount` 0..2, `params` `{seed, arm, pivot}` as before. | `{ok: true, rig}` (a copy of what was set), or `{ok: false, error: "unknown rig '<type>'"}` (also for inherited names like `toString`), or `{ok: false, error}` when `params` is given and isn't a plain object (a string, number, array); nothing changes then |
| `stage.openPhysicsPanel(id?)` | Selects object `id` when given, then opens the Physics panel (`physpanel.mjs` `open()`) for the selection. The App hands its panel to the stage as `stage.physicsPanel`. | `{ok: true, id}` (the selected id), or `{ok: false, error}` (unknown id, no panel, or `{ok: false, error: "select something"}` with no id and nothing selected: no empty panel is opened) |
| `stage.setSwim(id, mode)` | What a person (or a dog, cat, horse, bird) does in a pool, a tank or the sea ([buoyancy.mjs](buoyancy.mjs) `SWIM_MODES`): `auto` (swims as keyed: tread when still, breaststroke, freestyle), `tread`, `breaststroke`, `freestyle`, `back` (float on the back), `dive` (scuba, neutral: hovers at the keyed depth, mid-water when keyed on the floor), `flutter` / `frog` (underwater kicks), `cant` (Can't swim / not treading: struggles at the surface 2-3.5 s, then sinks slowly, limp, and settles on the bottom) or `sink` (unconscious: sinks slowly, no struggle). Case-insensitive; spoken words work too (`"can't swim"`, `"drowning"`, `"unconscious"`, `"float on back"`, `"scuba"`, `"tread water"`). `id` `null` = the selection. The old `swim: "off"` reads as `cant`. One undo step. Animals: `cant` / `sink` make them sink; the other modes keep them paddling. | `{ok: true, id, swim}` or `{ok: false, error}` (unknown id, not a person or an animal, unknown mode: nothing changes) |
| `stage.setDensity(id, value)` | The per-object **Floats / sinks** density (kg/m3, 20-20000), or a preset name (`foam` 100, `wood` 500, `low` 900, `neutral` 1000, `slow` 1100, `stone` 2600, `metal` 7850), or `"auto"` / `null` for the kind's own (or its Physics mass over its volume). Water is 1000. Saved as `actor.physics.density`; wins over the mass. One undo step. | `{ok: true, id, density, info}` (`info` as `buoyancyInfo`) or `{ok: false, error}` (junk, or a person on a swim plan: their Swim mode decides, see `swimInfo`; nothing changes) |
| `stage.buoyancyInfo(id?)` | How an object floats, read only: `{density, source ("kind" / "mass" / "density" / "sinking"), floats, under (fraction of its height under water when floating; 1 = sinks), terminal (m/s fully under: + sinks, - rises), what, soaks ({to, tau_s} or null), override}`. An animal set to `cant` / `sink` reports where it ends up (`floats: false`, `sinksAfter` s of paddling first). Also in `physicsInfo(id).buoyancy`. | the object, or `null` (unknown id, a boat: its hull has its own physics, a person on a swim plan: see `swimInfo`) |
| `stage.swimInfo(id?)` | What a person does in this set's water, in words, read only: `{swim (the mode), floats (stays up), stands (the water is shallow enough to stand in: depth <= 0.86 x their height, for `auto` / `cant`), what}`. Also in `physicsInfo(id).swim` (the Physics panel shows it in place of Floats / sinks). | the person, or `null` (no pool / tank / sea, not a person, a video-mocap person: they float by density) |
| `stage.setCameraCurve(mode)` | The active shot's camera curve: `"smooth"` (curve version 2, [camcurve.mjs](camcurve.mjs): no speed jumps at keys, focal in log space), `"even"` (version 2 at one even speed by arc length, camera and target travel combined; interior key times and holds are ignored) or `"legacy"` (the old curve shots saved before version 2 play). One undo step. `stage.cameraCurve()` reads it. | `{ok: true, curve}` or `{ok: false, error}` |

`setSwim`, `setDensity`, `buoyancyInfo` and `swimInfo` are tested in [tests/js/buoyancy.test.mjs](../../tests/js/buoyancy.test.mjs).

Tested in [tests/js/avatars_m15.test.mjs](../../tests/js/avatars_m15.test.mjs) ("Stage API for tools"): `placeInFrame` against a known camera (the projected point lands on the third at the asked distance, within 2 mm), a straight-down camera refusing 10 m, and `null`; `openPhysicsPanel` recording no undo step ("round 2" tests); `placeInFrame` with a preview camera on screen and with per-shot blocking, `setRig` with bad `params` and inherited names, `duplicate` of a person with per-shot blocking ("round 3" tests).

More in [tests/js/people_m16.test.mjs](../../tests/js/people_m16.test.mjs): near distances under a top-down and a steep camera, numeric strings and refused booleans / arrays, riding or carried objects refused, a raised platform floor (the point lands on the third at the platform's height; `key_y` kept for another shot's raised keys), and `openPhysicsPanel()` with nothing selected.
